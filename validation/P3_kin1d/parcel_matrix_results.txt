Adiabatic parcel matrix, 2026-10-15
=================================

Design: 2 initial temperatures (288,300 K) x 3 initial relative humidities
(60,80,100%) x 3 prescribed ascent rates (0.5,1,2 m/s), 18 paired cases.
All run for 5400 s, from p=101325 Pa with no condensate. No entrainment or
fallout; conserved liquid-ice potential temperature and total water.
Common pressure profile: p(z)=101325*(1-9.816*z/(1005*300))^(1005/287.15).
Final heights differ (2.7,5.4,10.8 km), so these are not fixed-height comparisons.

Actual production ParcelModel with SSP RK3, prognostic supersaturation, native
mixture thermodynamics, reference constants selected via public constructors.
No adapter or custom Breeze advancement. Fixed aerosol: 3e8 per kg dry air.
Process timescale tau=0.025 s throughout, including refinement. These are
controlled benchmark settings, not the example's native process defaults.
Upstream: directly compiled P3_MAIN, P3 5.5.2, existing pressure/theta_li host.
Neither CM1 nor kin1d is run. P3 source/patch provenance: see README.txt and
validation_output/p3_processes. Compiler: gfortran 16; Julia 1.12.7.

Baseline: Breeze dt=0.01 s; upstream dt=0.003125 s. Saved every 10 s.
Trajectory maxima below refer to those samples, not every internal step.
All 18 pairs finite; 127 physical checks passed. Maximum pressure mismatch
2.57e-9 Pa; theta_li drift zero; Breeze prognostic water drift zero; separately
summed Breeze water drift 3.47e-18 kg/kg; upstream water drift 3.48e-15 kg/kg.
No negative mass/number states. Supersaturation identity error <=3.39e-8 kg/kg.

Baseline findings:
  16/18 cases have maximum trajectory temperature difference <0.02 K.
  Case 3 (288 K,60%,2 m/s): 0.1388 K.
  Case 18 (300 K,100%,2 m/s): 0.3978 K.
  Maximum final-temperature difference across all cases: 0.004029 K.
  Maximum final cloud/rain/ice differences: 0.000724/0.002190/0.001407 g/kg.
  Worst mean absolute cloud/rain/ice trajectory differences, normalized by
  initial total water: 0.08597/0.06646/0.10913%. All occur in case 3.
  These small water-normalized metrics do not imply small relative errors
  in every individual species or accurate transition timing.
  Cold fast ascents (288 K,2 m/s) have ice-number discrepancies of
  29.59/34.70/53.20% of upstream peak at RH=60/80/100%, respectively.

Refinement:
  Cases 3,9,14,18: Breeze 0.01 -> 0.005; upstream 0.003125 -> 0.0015625 s.
  Cases 3,9,18 again: Breeze 0.005 -> 0.0025; upstream 0.0015625 -> 0.00078125 s.

Case 3 upstream has not demonstrated timestep convergence. Its temperature
discrepancy peaks near 1550 s during ice formation (266.80 vs 266.49 K).
Case 18 retains a short temperature discrepancy near 3580 s (272.74 vs
272.48 K) despite improved timestep sensitivity. Case 9 retains a large
ice-number discrepancy late in the trajectory despite much smaller changes
under the latest refinement; this is an unresolved implementation/numerical
formulation difference, not explained away by coarse timestep alone.
No process-code bug is diagnosed from these aggregate comparisons alone.

Interpretation: endpoint heatmaps and envelopes look very similar, but they
hide transient and number-concentration differences. Keep trajectory-error
and refinement views alongside them. This matrix is an exploratory comparison,
not proof of P3 equivalence or a converged reference across the whole design.
No agreement threshold was selected after observing the errors.

Changes in this experiment are validation setup, aggregation, and plotting
only. No production P3 formula, ParcelModel numerics, or default changed.
The old default benchmark's first 10 s match its archived output in a
regression check after parameterizing the initialization.

Reproduce using README.txt. Figures are large PNG and SVG files under
validation_output/p3_matrix/comparison_0.01_0.003125. Full raw data, per-case
errors, physical checks, and refinement measurements are retained locally.
The shaded trajectory envelopes are min-max over this finite factorial design;
they are not confidence intervals or probabilistic uncertainty estimates.
