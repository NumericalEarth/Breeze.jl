Production ParcelModel vs direct upstream P3 — 2026-10-15
========================================================

Outcome
-------
Actual ParcelModel reproduces the warm and cold trajectories closely, with
remaining differences in activation and number concentrations. This is not an
exact equivalence result or a demonstration of agreement with native default
process timescales. No new Breeze advancing driver was implemented.

Both runs use conserved liquid–ice potential temperature. ParcelModel uses its
existing SSP RK3 through Simulation/run!, native moist-air P3 equations, and
prognostic supersaturation. Only initialization writes supersaturation; subsequent
callbacks only save data. reference_conventions.jl is not imported into this run.
The compiled Fortran P3_MAIN reference uses its existing independent theta_li
host; its split stepping and saturation/number projections remain upstream-only.

Final comparison settings
-------------------------
Initial T=300 K, p=101325 Pa, water=0.015 kg/kg, no condensate; ascent at 1 or 2 m/s
for 5400 s. No sedimentation or entrainment. Fixed aerosol 3e8/kg dry air, converted
to 2.955e8/kg moist air. Pressure is the same prescribed isentropic profile,
sampled on 1 m centers by ParcelModel. Native mixture thermodynamics is retained.
Public constructors select reference gas/liquid/aerosol constants and Flatau
saturation; no custom thermodynamic methods or process formula overrides are used.

ParcelModel dt=0.00125 s; process relaxation/limiting tau=0.025 s. Upstream
dt=0.0015625 s. Tau stays fixed during ParcelModel timestep refinement. These
process timescales are numerical choices for this benchmark, not bug fixes or
changed package defaults. Upstream applies its own per-step limits, so the
comparison does not establish a shared continuum limit as tau approaches zero.
A native-defaults trajectory was also retained separately; it is not the closely
matching trajectory reported here.

Change classification
---------------------
1. Bug fix: microphysics received environmental density as if it were parcel
   density. Stored density-weighted variables now convert to the EOS parcel
   density for rate evaluation and back afterward. Storage, buoyancy, and RK3
   stepping are unchanged. Identical parcels with different environmental density
   weights have identical specific tendencies. In the controlled tau=10 case,
   this alone reduced warm-case maximum temperature error from 0.603 to 0.0356 K.
   File: src/ParcelModels/parcel_dynamics.jl, compute_parcel_tendencies!.

2. Bug fix: cloud-number transfers formed N/(rho*q) before multiplying by a mass
   rate. The ratio could overflow for subnormal cloud water, yielding Inf*0=NaN.
   The algebraically equivalent evaluation (N/rho)*(mass_rate/q) avoids that
   intermediate. No water cutoff or modified physical coefficient was introduced.
   Files: process_rate_helpers.jl and its rain/riming/prognostic call sites.

3. Coupling correction with an explicit numerical reformulation: the parcel
   supersaturation RHS now includes its prescribed pressure change and uses the
   chain rule for s=qv-e_sat(T)*Rm/(p*Rv), with actual phase-change tendencies and
   the selected conserved thermodynamic variable. This replaces a finite-tau
   end-state estimate with an instantaneous RHS on the parcel path. It should be
   reviewed separately from the two algebraic/coupling bugs above; it changes the
   ODE supplied to RK3. Supersaturation is never projected or reset during a run.
   The pressure derivative follows the same piecewise-linear interpolant used by
   the parcel. Analytic saturation derivatives use the selected saturation law.
   Files: p3_parcel.jl and the three Thermodynamics saturation-law derivatives.

4. Validation configuration only: matching documented public constants, fixed
   aerosol, smaller process timescales, fine timesteps, and read-only conversion
   of upstream dry-basis output. These changes do not alter package defaults.

The earlier changes honoring ParcelDynamics' thermodynamic formulation and fixing
static-energy latent-heat reference offsets remain separate concerns. They were
already present before this production-comparison work. The static-energy fix is
PR 1080; formulation support corresponds to PR 1075. This working tree also
contains earlier example and documentation edits, not all part of the new fixes.

Accuracy evidence and limits
----------------------------
The native RK3 implementation is unchanged. Independent finite differences verify
the full supersaturation chain rule for liquid and mixed-phase states under both
static energy and theta_li. A smooth dry-ascent convergence check gives errors
5.055e-13, 3.156e-14, and 1.960e-15 kg/kg at dt=20, 10, and 5 s: no observed loss of
integration order in this check. It is not an order guarantee across P3 switches.

Halving ParcelModel dt from 0.0025 to 0.00125 s changes cold-case temperature by at
most 0.000184 K and ice number by 0.098% of peak (0.087% normalized time-integrated
absolute difference). Halving upstream dt from 0.003125 to 0.0015625 s changes
cold temperature by 0.000818 K and ice number by 0.092% of peak. The original
0.025 s upstream trajectory substantially under-resolved the nucleation burst:
peak ice number was about 267000/kg dry air, versus 318000 on the finer runs.
It would have been incorrect to tune ParcelModel to that coarse trajectory.

Remaining cloud-number peak-normalized differences are 15–17%; in the 0.0025 s
run, differences above 5% occupy one saved 10 s sample near activation. Rain-number
time-integrated differences are about 3.1–3.4%. Physical conventions (including
rounded upstream epsilon=0.622), native limiters/projections, and finite relaxation
remain different. No individual remaining discrepancy is attributed to a specific
cause without a discriminating test. These two ascents do not validate every P3
process or GPU execution.
