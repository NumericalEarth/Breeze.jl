Liquid–ice potential-temperature results, 2026-10-15
==================================================

Default behavior
----------------
AtmosphereModel's public default was already :LiquidIcePotentialTemperature.
ParcelDynamics ignored it. The local formulation-selection fix (also proposed in
PR #1075) now materializes, initializes, and advances the requested state, including
the default. The DCMIP Kessler parcel update no longer assumes a static_energy field.
No production P3 process equations were changed for this rerun.

Production examples
-------------------
Reran prescribed, dry, and buoyant parcels using liquid–ice potential temperature,
and the 3600-second P3 kinematic column (which already used that formulation).
PNG figures have 2000–2400-pixel widths; SVG versions are included.
Outputs: validation_output/p3_liquid_ice/{parcels,kinematic}.
Full parcel trajectories and a measured conservation report accompany the figures.

Direct P3 with a liquid–ice host
-------------------------------
Same 12 closed boxes and two 90-minute ascents as the previous direct suite.
Same source, process constants, table data, and acceptance tolerances. Each host
conserves theta_li and reconstructs temperature from pressure and phase fractions.
The Fortran host equation is independent of Breeze's thermodynamic implementation.
See README.txt for the precise adapter and operator-splitting scope.

All 227 trajectory checks pass at dt=0.05 s and again at dt=0.025 s. These include
all 14 cases, conservation of total water and theta_li in both implementations,
finite/nonnegative states, a 0.001 K maximum temperature error, and maximum species
errors of 0.5% of each reference peak plus a 1e-10 absolute trace allowance.

At dt=0.05 s:
  Warm 1 m/s ascent: maximum temperature difference 1.06881e-6 K.
  Cold 2 m/s ascent: maximum temperature difference 0.000408072 K.
  Cold ascent maximum differences, normalized by reference peak:
    cloud liquid 0.01145%; rain 0.003515%; frozen ice 0.0009792%;
    cloud number 0.008344%; ice number 0.01531%.

At dt=0.025 s:
  Warm 1 m/s ascent: maximum temperature difference 1.07858e-6 K.
  Cold 2 m/s ascent: maximum temperature difference 0.000414221 K.
  Cold ascent: cloud liquid 0.01144%; rain 0.003513%; frozen ice 0.0009942%;
               cloud number 0.008347%; ice number 0.01099%.

Timestep sensitivity remains important. At 0.1 s the cold-ascent cloud number has
one brief 4.826%-of-peak discrepancy; 226/227 checks pass, so that timestep is not
accepted for this suite. No tolerances were relaxed. Refining 0.05 to 0.025 s changes
the reference cold-ascent temperature by at most 0.00679 K and frozen-ice mass by
0.0363% of peak, but ice number by 7.52% of peak (6.64% integrated L1). Breeze has
nearly the same sensitivity. Agreement between implementations is established at
two timesteps; complete timestep convergence of ice number is not established.

These results validate the matched P3 operator and liquid–ice host reconstruction.
They are not a claim that native kin1d, this split driver, and production RK3
ParcelDynamics produce identical trajectories with their different default options.

Additional checks
-----------------
The production dry, prescribed-P3, and buoyant-P3 parcel trajectories pass all 12
conservation/type/finiteness checks. Measured theta_li drift is exactly zero;
maximum total-water drift is 1.73472e-18 kg/kg in the two cloudy cases.
The complete direct suite passes 433 one-step checks, 199 original reference-host
trajectory checks, and 227 liquid–ice-host checks at each of two timesteps.
P3 atmosphere integration (170), routing (66), and trace-regime (34) regressions,
static-energy phase-change checks (108), and five ExplicitImports checks also pass.
Parcel default/formulation, atmosphere construction, and saturation regressions
also pass (386 checks, including Float32/Float64 model construction).
