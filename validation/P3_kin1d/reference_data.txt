P3 parcel reference data v1.0
============================

These are direct official-P3 runs, NOT CM1 output. P3_MAIN is called by the
Fortran host in trajectory_reference.jl. The host prescribes pressure and
conserves liquid-ice potential temperature. Sedimentation is disabled and
prognostic supersaturation enabled. Native P3 process updates, clipping, and
per-step adjustments are retained. Diagnostic interventions that alter these
policies are NOT included here.

Generation scripts/results: Breeze.jl validation/P3_kin1d at c0100340.
The provenance directory contains the baseline source patch and compiler version;
each trajectory directory contains its exact Fortran host. Reproduce with
process_reference.jl, trajectory_reference.jl, and outlier_reference.jl baseline,
as described in validation/P3_kin1d/README.txt. package_reference.jl packages the
existing output and does not rerun or advance any model. No executables included.

All trajectories: 5400 s; p0=101325 Pa; initially no condensate; fixed aerosol
3e8 per kg dry air; no entrainment or fallout. Pressure at height z is
101325 * (1 - 9.816*z/(1005*300))^(1005/287.15), with z=w*t in meters.

matrix/cases.dat gives case_id, T0 [K], RH0 [fraction], w [m/s], and total
water [kg/kg moist air]. RH is vapor pressure divided by Flatau liquid saturation
pressure; initial vapor uses epsilon=0.622. The matrix spans T0=(288,300) K,
RH0=(0.6,0.8,1), and w=(0.5,1,2) m/s. Final heights therefore differ.
reference_dt0.003125 contains all 18 cases; dt0.0015625 contains IDs 3,9,14,18;
dt0.00078125 contains IDs 3,9,18. Directory names give timesteps in seconds.

two_ascents contains T0=300 K, total water=0.015 kg/kg moist air. IDs 13 and 14
ascend at 1 and 2 m/s respectively. The host's cases.dat also lists unused
process-test initial states; its explicit test>=13 branch sets the parcel water.
These controlled benchmarks use reference constants; they are not automatically
equivalent to the native-default simulations in examples/rising_parcels.jl.

reference_final.dat schema (whitespace-separated; no header; every 10 s,
starting at t=10 s, so initialization comes from the case definitions):
  1 case ID
  2 time [s]
  3 pressure [Pa]
  4 temperature [K]
  5 vapor mixing ratio [kg/kg dry air]
  6 cloud liquid mixing ratio [kg/kg dry air]
  7 cloud number [1/kg dry air]
  8 rain mixing ratio [kg/kg dry air]
  9 rain number [1/kg dry air]
 10 frozen ice mixing ratio, excluding liquid coating [kg/kg dry air]
 11 ice number [1/kg dry air]
 12 rime mass, already included in frozen ice [kg/kg dry air]
 13 rime volume [m^3/kg dry air]
 14 liquid coating [kg/kg dry air]
 15 liquid supersaturation, rv - rv_sat [kg/kg dry air]

To convert columns 5:14 to moist-air quantities, multiply by the dry fraction
qd = 1/(1 + col5 + col6 + col8 + col10 + col14). Do not count rime twice.
For column 15, use qd * epsilon/(epsilon + rv_sat) * col15, epsilon=0.622,
rv_sat=col5-col15. See plot_parcel_matrix.jl for the conversion.

event_diagnostics contains read-only 0.1 s process-entry samples around the
three outliers, using dt=0.00078125 s. rate_columns.txt lists the first ten
columns on one line, then the names of the remaining 35 rates, one per line.
These are process-entry states, staggered by less than 0.001 s from completed
Breeze states. Ice mass here includes coating (unlike reference_final.dat).
intervention.patch in this directory adds instrumentation ONLY; its 10 s
trajectory is bit-for-bit identical to the non-instrumented reference.

Limits: 10 s trajectory samples miss fast freezing peaks. High-resolution
diagnostics reveal transient differences as large as 2.96 K. Small-timestep
convergence is affected by per-call trace cleanup and instantaneous nucleation
adjustment. This dataset is a versioned comparison reference, not a claim of
physical truth or full numerical equivalence. Read outlier_results.txt before
interpreting errors. The corrected aerosol R=8.3145 benchmark setting affects
Breeze only; the official-P3 reference already used that value.
