# Breeze parcel benchmarks for droplet activation and growth

Breeze thermodynamics (default constants unless noted), `condensational_growth_step` (implicit), no ventilation, operator splitting of expansion and condensation. Generated by `parcel_benchmarks.jl`.

## 1. Rogers (1975), Fig. 1: N = 200 cm⁻³, r₀ = 8 μm, 800 hPa, 280.15 K, 𝒮₀ = 0, w = 10 m/s

Reference: Rogers' figure (digitized in CloudMicrophysics.jl): 𝒮max = 0.978% at 7.16 s (0.963% at 9.79 s), r(19.9 s) = 9.68 μm.
PySDM (Rogers' equations, LSODA) asserts 𝒮max = 0.97% at 7 ± 0.5 s.

|   + fixed N per volume | 1.058 | 7.78 | 9.66 |
