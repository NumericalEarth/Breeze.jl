ENA reference data for 18 July 2017

Source: U.S. Department of Energy Atmospheric Radiation Measurement (ARM) User Facility.
Accessed: 15 October 2026.

LASSO-ENA data were compiled by WI Gustafson, SE Giangrande, S Endo,
J Rausch, H Xiao, and D Zhang. Location: Graciosa Island, Azores, Portugal,
Central Facility C1 (39.0916 N, 28.0257 W).

LASSO-ENA dataset: https://doi.org/10.5439/2572639
SAM domain statistics: https://doi.org/10.5439/2572635
SAM inputs: https://doi.org/10.5439/2572661
Documentation: https://doi.org/10.5439/2572640
Citation guidance: https://lasso-ena.svcs.arm.gov/latest/citing_LASSO.html

The SAM statistics subsets retain time, z, CWP, RWP, TABS, QV, U, and V
at all original times and heights. Values and units are unchanged. Files were
repacked with NetCDF compression; unused variables and internal storage
metadata were omitted. Original simulation identifiers and scientific metadata
are retained. Source filenames are recorded in comparison_source.txt and in
each subset's subset_source_file attribute.

The four simulations use SAM v6.10.3 with LASSO modifications and Morrison
microphysics: 20170718<forcing>d25x100_morr-aer2-flxsst, for forcing =
era5, era5s1n0, merra2, and merra2s1n0. They are existing ARM simulations.
They are not Breeze simulations and do not have identical configurations to
the accompanying Breeze example.

The archived SAM input files snd, lsf, sfc, prm, and grd are copied unchanged.
Their original ARM README files and SHA-256 checksums accompany them.
No SAM executable, model source, or microphysics lookup tables are included.

The observations are copied unchanged from these ARM datastreams:
  enamwrret2turnC1.c1.20170718.031053.nc
  enasondewnpnC1.b1.20170718.113800.cdf
Their original netCDF metadata identify data processing, contacts, and sources.
MWRRET v2 liquid-water retrieval: https://doi.org/10.5439/1566156
The MWRRET file credits David Turner for the retrieval algorithm and AER, Inc.
for the MonoRTM forward model; those acknowledgments are retained in the file.
